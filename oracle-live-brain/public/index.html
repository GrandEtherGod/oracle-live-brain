    How to read it

    Anticipation

    When a turn starts, the pathways that faculty is likely to use glow gold, before anything fires.

    Confirmation

    When the turn finishes, the pathways that fired flash, and the rest fall quiet.

    Echo

    The finished turn sends gold pulses back into older pathways and strengthens them. It is a picture of influence. Nothing in the record moves backward in time.

    Voice

    Replies come from a model that is given the brain’s real numbers, then spoken with the browser voice. With no key set, a fixed template speaks those same numbers.
